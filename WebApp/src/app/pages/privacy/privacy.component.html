SponsPay Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SponsPay collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when people visit the SponsPay website, use SponsPay products and services, send or receive Premium Messages, register as creators, contact support, or otherwise interact with SponsPay.

2. Information we collect

SponsPay may collect information such as names, usernames, display names, email addresses, phone numbers, creator channel information, payout information, billing and transaction data, device and browser data, usage logs, message content, support communications, and information from connected services or integrations that are necessary to provide the service.

3. How we use information

SponsPay uses information to operate and improve the service; process payments and payouts; deliver Premium Messages; notify creators and supporters; manage statuses, replies, and support workflows; detect fraud and abuse; enforce policies; communicate with users; comply with legal obligations; and analyze service performance.

4. Information from third parties and integrations

SponsPay may receive information from payment providers, messaging providers, authentication or platform integrations, analytics providers, and service partners when that is necessary to operate the service.

5. How we share information

SponsPay may share information with service providers and processors, payment and payout partners, messaging and infrastructure providers, professional advisers, law enforcement or regulators where legally required, and counterparties involved in corporate transactions. SponsPay does not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Creator and supporter visibility

Information may be visible to other service participants where necessary to provide the product. For example, a creator may see the content and metadata associated with a supporter's Premium Message, and a supporter may see message-status updates. SponsPay should disclose the relevant visibility rules in product flows.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

SponsPay may use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies for authentication, security, analytics, performance, preferences, and service improvement. SponsPay should provide any required consent or preference mechanisms based on the jurisdictions in which it operates.

8. Data retention

SponsPay retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may differ by data category and legal requirement.

9. Security

SponsPay uses reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, and SponsPay cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing

SponsPay may process information in countries other than the country where a user is located. Where required, SponsPay should implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

11. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of certain information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. SponsPay may need to verify identity before acting on a request.

12. Children

SponsPay is not intended for children below the age required by applicable law to use the service. SponsPay may remove accounts, data, or content where child-safety or age-compliance concerns arise.

13. Changes to this policy

SponsPay may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. SponsPay should post the revised version and update the effective date when changes are made.

14. Contact

SponsPay should publish a current privacy contact email and any legally required mailing details before this policy goes live.
