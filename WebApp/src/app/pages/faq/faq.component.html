SponsPay FAQ

For supporters

What is a Premium Message?

A Premium Message is a paid message sent through SponsPay in connection with a creator, video, livestream, topic, or other supported creator interaction.

What happens after I send a Premium Message?

SponsPay processes your payment, delivers the message into the creator's priority flow, notifies the creator, and shows you status updates.

Does the creator always reply personally?

No. Creator-personal replies are encouraged, but they are not guaranteed for every message unless a stronger promise is explicitly sold for a particular message tier.

What if the creator does not personally reply?

If the creator does not personally reply within the disclosed service window, SponsPay may send a platform-managed response.

Is a SponsPay-managed response the same thing as a creator reply?

No. SponsPay-managed responses are labeled separately so you know whether a response came from the creator or from SponsPay.

Do I need Telegram to pay?

No. Telegram should not be required for payment. Where available, Telegram is an optional post-payment channel for deeper community interaction, updates, and supporter engagement.

When can I get a refund?

Refunds may be available if SponsPay fails to deliver the message correctly, fails to provide the promised service workflow within the disclosed window, or if the message is cancelled or rejected under policy.

Can my message be rejected?

Yes. Messages may be rejected, removed, or refunded if they violate the rules, contain prohibited content, appear fraudulent, or cannot be fulfilled safely.

Can I edit or cancel a message after paying?

SponsPay may allow limited post-purchase changes in some cases, but accepted messages may move quickly into creator workflows. The exact rules should be stated on the message or checkout flow.

How do I contact support?

Use the support or contact method shown in the SponsPay product, on the relevant creator page, or on the SponsPay website.

For creators

How do creators make money on SponsPay?

Creators earn a share of eligible Premium Message revenue. The exact share may depend on the creator's response performance and other program conditions.

Can creators earn a higher revenue share by replying more consistently?

Yes. SponsPay uses a performance-based revenue-share model in which better creator-personal reply performance can unlock higher revenue-share bands.

What counts toward a creator's reply performance?

Creator-personal replies that can be verified through SponsPay-approved channels or auditable events count. Platform-managed fallback responses do not count toward the creator's top performance tier.

Does simply being a Telegram admin prove that a creator saw a message?

No. SponsPay does not rely on passive Telegram admin status alone as proof that a creator saw a specific message.

What happens if I do not reply in time?

SponsPay may send a platform-managed fallback response, adjust payout handling, and count the message as unanswered for creator-performance purposes.

Why does SponsPay use Telegram?

Telegram is an owned supporter surface and creator-notification layer that can support repeat engagement, supporter updates, and additional creator interaction when creators choose to participate.

Where can I see my message deadlines and earnings?

Creators should see message statuses, deadlines, and payout states in the creator portal.

Does SponsPay charge upfront creator fees?

The current model is designed so that SponsPay earns a share when the creator earns revenue, but exact pricing and payout terms should always be confirmed in the creator agreement and portal.
