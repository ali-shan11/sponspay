Premium Message Response and Refund Policy

1. Purpose

This policy explains what SponsPay promises when a supporter pays for a Premium Message, how replies and platform-managed responses work, and when refunds may be available.

2. What SponsPay promises by default

  • SponsPay will process the payment for an accepted Premium Message.
  • SponsPay will deliver the message into the creator's priority SponsPay flow.
  • SponsPay will notify the creator through the configured workflow.
  • SponsPay will provide message-status tracking.
  • If the creator does not personally reply within the disclosed service window, SponsPay may send a platform-managed response.

3. What SponsPay does not promise by default

  • A personal creator reply for every message.
  • Proof that a creator passively viewed a message in Telegram.
  • The same response time or response style for every creator, market, or message type.

4. Service window

Unless SponsPay clearly discloses a different timing for a particular product tier, the default service window for creator-personal reply or platform-managed fallback response is 14 days from successful submission.

5. Platform-managed responses

If the creator does not personally reply within the service window, SponsPay may send a platform-managed response. SponsPay will label that response in a way that does not misrepresent it as a creator-personal reply.

6. Refund eligibility

  • SponsPay may issue a refund if SponsPay fails to deliver an accepted Premium Message correctly.
  • SponsPay may issue a refund if SponsPay fails to provide the promised service workflow within the disclosed service window.
  • SponsPay may issue a refund if the message is rejected, cancelled, duplicated, or cannot be fulfilled safely or lawfully.
  • SponsPay may deny a refund where the service was completed according to the disclosed workflow, including where a clearly labeled platform-managed response was provided within the service window.

7. Prohibited or unsafe messages

SponsPay may refuse, remove, or refund messages that violate product rules, community standards, law, or safety requirements, or that create material operational or reputational risk.

8. How to request support

Users should use the support channels identified in the product, creator page, or website. SponsPay may request order details, creator details, and message details in order to review the request.
