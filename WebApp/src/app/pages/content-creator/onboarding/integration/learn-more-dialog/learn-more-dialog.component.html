How Telegram Integration Works

SponsPay connects your audience to you through a dedicated private Telegram channel.

Your Premium Channel

When you create a channel handle, SponsPay sets up a private Telegram channel dedicated to your audience. You'll be added as a co-administrator, giving you the ability to post, edit, and delete messages in your channel.

Premium Fan Messages

Fans can send you paid messages through your SponsPay link. Once payment is confirmed, their message is automatically delivered to your Telegram channel. You can read and reply to fan messages directly in Telegram.

Managed & Secure

SponsPay manages the channel infrastructure so you can focus on your content. Payment processing, message delivery, and failed payment refunds are all handled automatically. Your channel is private, so only paid fan messages appear there.

Getting Started

  1. Choose a handle for your channel or enter a custom one
  2. Scan the QR code with your Telegram app to join your channel
  3. Get promoted automatically to co-admin within seconds
  4. Share your SponsPay link with your audience to start receiving messages
